# GDB (GNU Debugger)

GDB is the first tool I open after checking a file's type. It is the industry standard for debugging and disassembly, providing an essential window into how a binary behaves at runtime.

## My Perspective
GDB becomes significantly more powerful when paired with plugins like pwndbg, GEF, or PEDA, which provide a much-needed visual representation of the registers and stack. The biggest hurdle is the learning curve; mastering it can be confusing at first because everything happens in the terminal, requiring a strong grasp of the underlying system.

## Verdict:
Difficult to master, but absolutely mandatory for anyone serious about pwn and binary analysis.
