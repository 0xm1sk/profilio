# Nmap

You cannot claim to be in cybersecurity without knowing Nmap. It is the primary key to any network, providing the initial visibility needed for any operation.

## My Perspective
Nmap is amazing for its versatility. Beyond just scanning for open, closed, or filtered ports, its ability to execute scripts (NSE) for vulnerability detection makes it an all-in-one reconnaissance tool. It transforms a blind target into a mapped attack surface.

## Verdict:
The definitive tool for network discovery.
