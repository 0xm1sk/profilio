# Metasploit

Metasploit is the kind of tool every researcher should have in their arsenal. It automates the tedious parts of exploitation, allowing you to focus on the target rather than the boilerplate code.

## My Perspective
It is a massive time-saver for verifying vulnerabilities; you simply specify the parameters and execute. However, in modern Red Teaming or professional pentesting, I use it sparingly. Because it is so widely known, its payloads are highly detected by EDR and AV systems, and it lacks the stealthy evasion techniques needed for sophisticated targets.

## Verdict:
A well-maintained, essential framework for rapid testing and learning.
